# AI Usage Disclosure Practice

Human maintainers remain responsible for code, claims, citations, rights, privacy, and scientific interpretation. AI systems are not authors.

For materially AI-assisted research, code, documentation, translation, review, or testing, record when relevant:

- tool/model or service identity when known;
- date or version window;
- task performed;
- human review performed;
- whether generated material was accepted, modified, or rejected;
- whether private or third-party data was sent to an external service;
- links to affected public commits or research artifacts where useful.

For a future paper or archival release, disclose AI use according to the venue's then-current requirements. Historical uncertainty should be disclosed rather than replaced by invented precision.
