# Deliberative Edition — research notes

This branch is intended to support future engineering and academic work around **provenance-bound dynamic adjudication**.

Potential research abstractions exposed by the implementation include:

1. **Bounded open-world discovery** — open semantic discovery followed by independent inventory review, temporary freeze, explicit gap detection and reopen.
2. **Argument role-state dynamics** — a source-grounded pressure can persist while its argumentative role changes.
3. **Higher-order semantic composition** — relations among SC candidates are inferred semantically rather than by fixed branch counts or keyword rules.
4. **Adjudicative consequence reconciliation** — local argumentative success is kept distinct from motion-level consequence.
5. **Dependency-aware deduplication** — multiple presentation views of one causal dependency do not become multiple votes.
6. **Role-separated epistemic authority** — semantic generation, review, projection, fidelity and publication are separated and version-bound.

These are research hypotheses / system-design contributions, not claims of priority over all prior literature.
