# Research Impact Evidence

Status: **evidence tracker; no qualifying JOSS impact claim is currently asserted**.

JOSS expects software to have a research role beyond prospective usefulness. This file should accumulate verifiable evidence after the repository is public.

## Evidence categories

Record only real events, with dates and durable references where possible:

| Category | Examples of acceptable evidence | Current entry |
|---|---|---|
| Research use by the authors | software used in a paper/preprint, registered study, corpus/annotation workflow, or documented experiment | Not yet entered |
| External research use | another researcher/group uses the software in analysis, teaching-research, annotation, or a published/preprint workflow | Not yet entered |
| Scholarly citation | software/repository/release cited in a paper, preprint, thesis, or technical report | Not yet entered |
| Research integration | integration into a reproducible research pipeline or public research artifact | Not yet entered |
| Community research contribution | substantive research-oriented issues, extensions, validation reports, or contributions from outside the maintainer | Not yet entered |

## Recording template

For each future item record:

- date;
- evidence category;
- software version/commit;
- who performed the use (when public and appropriate);
- research question/workflow;
- durable URL/DOI/citation if available;
- what the evidence establishes;
- what it **does not** establish.

Do not count repository stars, ordinary downloads, synthetic smoke tests, internal red-team notes, private design freezes, or self-authored claims as independent scientific validation.

## Separation from scientific performance

Research impact and adjudication quality are different evidence classes. External use does not prove correctness; software tests do not prove human agreement; a paper citation does not imply benchmark superiority. Each claim must keep its own evidence contract.
