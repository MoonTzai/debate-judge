# Debate-Judge · Deliberative Edition

**Deliberative Edition（审议版）** is the V10-R2.1 research line of Debate-Judge.

Its research focus is not merely detecting argument relations, but governing how source-grounded semantic interpretations become an auditable final adjudication.

Core mechanisms in this branch:

- whole-debate semantic discovery;
- independent reviewed inventory and explicit freeze/reopen boundaries;
- role-state, accepted pressure, B′ and dependency-aware SC representation;
- higher-order / parallel / partial-composition reasoning without keyword or branch-count rules;
- separation of semantic authority from exact-source projection;
- local-clash truth versus motion-level adjudicative consequence;
- causal-dependency deduplication before final adjudication;
- staged semantic checkpoints, revision binding, CAS-style publication and fail-closed recovery;
- source/provenance binding and browser/runtime reproducibility checks.

The branch is derived from the frozen **V10优化版定版-20260924** baseline.

Private debate transcripts, raw provider/API Flight recordings, historical private cutover state, and debate-specific regression material are intentionally not part of this public branch.

See [deliberative-reproducibility.md](deliberative-reproducibility.md) for reproducibility boundaries and commands.
